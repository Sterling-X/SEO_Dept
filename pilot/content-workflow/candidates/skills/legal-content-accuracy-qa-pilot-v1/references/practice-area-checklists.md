# Practice-Area-Specific Checklists

Apply the relevant checklist(s) below in addition to the Steps 1 through 9 protocol. If the page spans more than one practice area, apply each applicable list. Every item checked here still follows the live-verification mandate: confirm against current primary authority fetched during the review, and log it.

---

## Family law

- Residency and waiting periods
- Best-interest factors
- Parenting-time and decision-making terminology
- Support eligibility versus support calculation
- Income definitions
- Modification standards
- Temporary versus final orders
- Statutory presumptions and burdens
- Marital agreement enforceability
- Jurisdiction-specific terms for custody, maintenance, placement, or time-sharing

---

## Estate planning, probate, and guardianship

- Correct role names
- Venue
- Standing
- Petition contents
- Medical or evaluation-report requirements
- Notice and service
- Hearing deadlines
- Burden and standard of proof
- Temporary or emergency procedures
- Order, oath, bond, letters, training, reporting, and accounting requirements
- Limited versus plenary authority
- Least-restrictive-alternative requirements

---

## Criminal law

- Offense elements
- Classification
- Sentencing ranges
- Enhancements
- Defenses
- Expungement or sealing eligibility
- Arrest versus conviction consequences
- Mandatory versus discretionary penalties
- State versus federal jurisdiction

---

## Personal injury

- Statutes of limitation and repose
- Governmental-notice requirements
- Comparative or contributory negligence
- Damage caps
- No-fault thresholds
- Wrongful-death beneficiaries
- Medical-malpractice screening or affidavit rules
- Accrual and discovery rules

---

## Employment law

- Employee versus independent-contractor standards
- State and federal coverage thresholds
- Filing deadlines
- Administrative exhaustion
- Protected classes
- Wage-and-hour exemptions
- State-specific leave, scheduling, and pay-transparency rules

---

## Immigration

- Current federal statutes, regulations, and agency guidance
- Forms, filing fees, and editions
- Processing rules and policy changes
- Treat all timelines and agency procedures as time-sensitive
