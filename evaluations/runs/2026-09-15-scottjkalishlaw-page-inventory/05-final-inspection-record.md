## Deterministic checks

For included destinations, the strategist checked live HTTP/final URL behavior, canonical declaration, robots declaration, title or heading/purpose, and source discovery. Every final URL returned `200`, resolved at the listed URL, declared a self-canonical, and declared `index,follow` at inspection time. These checks do not establish actual Google indexation.

The English and Spanish navigation were compared with XML and human sitemap inventories. Navigation received priority, while substantial sitemap/internal-link omissions and reviewer-surfaced landing-page variants were evaluated individually. Redirect sources were not retained as separate dashboard URLs.

This is one evaluated inventory and one review cycle. Agreement on retained rows is not evidence of general SEO competence or a completed performance evaluation.
