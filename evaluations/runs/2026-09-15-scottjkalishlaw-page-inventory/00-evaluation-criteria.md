# Fixed Evaluation Criteria

Run: `2026-09-15-scottjkalishlaw-page-inventory`  
Business: `https://scottjkalishlaw.com/`  
Frozen: 2026-09-15 before target-site research

These criteria remain fixed while comparing the strategist's initial answer with the reviewer-informed revision. New evidence may change a page decision, but not the criteria used to judge it.

## Task outcome

Identify the site's main practice-area pages and other justified commercial or authority-building pages for an SEO dashboard. Include a location page only when the inspected evidence supports a physical office with a name, street address, and phone number. Produce a plain canonical URL list, a separate evidence table, and a short inspection record.

## Criteria

| Criterion | Passing condition |
|---|---|
| URL validity and canonical destination where verifiable | The URL resolves successfully in the available live check and is represented by its final or declared canonical destination. Redirect-only and duplicate variants are excluded. Any check blocked by access is labeled unavailable. |
| Appropriate commercial or authority intent | The page has a substantial practice/service, consultation/conversion, attorney/firm trust, physical-office, or durable authority purpose relevant to organic visibility. Commercial importance is labeled as an inference unless conversion or revenue evidence is available. |
| Compliance with exclusions | Blog posts, news articles, irrelevant utility pages, duplicate URLs, redirect variants, and general service-area pages lacking verified office evidence are excluded. |
| Evidence for physical-office classification | The included page provides an identifiable office/location name or firm identity, a complete street address, and a phone number, and the inspected evidence describes or corroborates it as an actual office rather than service-area coverage. If that threshold is not met, the page is not classified as a physical-office page. |
| Coverage of inspected navigation and sitemap | The record identifies the navigation, sitemap source, and relevant internal links actually inspected. Main navigation-linked pages are prioritized. Meaningful sitemap or internal-link omissions are considered and dispositioned. |
| Evidence for inclusion and exclusion decisions | Every included page has a cited source and reason. Material exclusions or ambiguous candidates have a recorded source, decision, and uncertainty instead of an unsupported guess. |

## Fixed decision rules

1. Prefer the final HTTPS URL and consistent host/trailing-slash form shown by live redirects or canonical markup when verifiable.
2. Treat navigation placement as strong prioritization evidence, not proof of conversions or revenue.
3. Treat a dedicated practice-area or service page as commercial-intent evidence; describe its business importance as an inference without performance data.
4. Treat attorney biographies, about/firm pages, results/testimonials, and durable resource pages as authority-building only when their substance and site role justify dashboard monitoring.
5. Do not infer a physical office from a city name, service-area claim, footer list, or location-specific practice page alone.
6. Prefer first-party page content for page purpose and canonical evidence. Use an authoritative professional, governmental, or business-listing source to corroborate an office when available.
7. When robots, rendering, authentication, rate limits, or tool limitations prevent a check, record the limitation and do not convert missing evidence into a positive finding.
8. Preserve the initial answer unchanged after it is written. Give the reviewer the frozen criteria and source inventory before revealing that initial answer.

## Evaluation boundary

This pilot measures request compliance, evidence quality, classification accuracy, and material omissions for one live-site task. It is not proof of general SEO competence or of the reviewer's effectiveness.
