# Local SEO Reference

Use this file for Google Business Profile strategy, local pack visibility, location pages, service areas, reviews, citations, and multi-location systems.

## 1) Local SEO model

Think in three practical buckets:

- **Relevance**: how clearly the business and landing page match the service + area
- **Prominence**: how trusted, reviewed, and cited the business appears
- **Coverage / geography**: how well the business serves or is associated with the area

## 2) GBP workflow

Check:
- primary and secondary categories
- business description and service fit
- products/services completeness
- photos and update cadence
- Q&A
- attributes
- hours and holiday accuracy
- review volume, recency, and response behavior
- appointment / conversion setup
- location-to-landing-page alignment

## 3) Location page rules

A good location page includes:
- unique local relevance
- clear service + geography match
- local proof (reviews, photos, staff, case studies, jobs, projects, landmarks, certifications)
- strong CTA
- nearby-area references where natural
- internal links to services and neighboring locations
- local schema when appropriate

Avoid:
- city-swap boilerplate
- near-duplicate service-area pages
- pages with no local proof or real purpose

## 4) Multi-location systems

For multi-location brands, design:
- a repeatable but not duplicate location-page template
- standardized GBP management
- review request and response workflow
- local link / community outreach process
- location-level KPI reporting
- owner mapping for operations vs marketing

## 5) Review systems

Recommendations should cover:
- how to ask compliantly
- how to improve response rate
- how to respond to positive and negative reviews
- how to route recurring complaints into ops fixes
- how to use review language in copy and FAQs

## 6) Citations

Treat citations as hygiene, not the entire strategy.
Use them to:
- clean up major inconsistency issues
- support new locations
- reinforce trust where relevant

Do not rely on citation blasting as a primary growth lever.

## 7) Local links and prominence

Good local authority sources:
- chambers and associations
- local sponsorships
- community orgs
- local media
- partner pages
- schools and nonprofits
- event pages
- neighborhood guides and reputable directories

## 8) Service-area businesses

For SABs:
- be careful with fake-address patterns
- focus on real service coverage and proof
- use location + service pages intentionally
- align GBP service areas with real operations
- support priority markets with local content and links

## 9) Deliverables

This module should produce:
- local SEO audit
- GBP optimization plan
- location-page template
- review system workflow
- multi-location operating plan
- local link acquisition list
