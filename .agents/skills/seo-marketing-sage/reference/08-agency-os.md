# Agency OS Reference

Use this file for audits, 30/60/90 plans, owner mapping, retainer structure, deliverables, and SEO operating cadence.

## 1) Discovery structure

At kickoff, define:
- business goals
- revenue model
- target audience
- priority markets
- primary conversion event
- top page types
- top constraints
- available team and owners
- existing data sources
- success metrics

## 2) Audit structure

A good audit should include:
- executive summary
- growth thesis
- findings by layer
- severity and effort
- prioritized actions
- dependencies and owners
- 30/60/90 roadmap
- KPI plan

Common audit layers:
- market + intent
- content + page strategy
- technical
- internal linking + IA
- authority / trust
- local
- analytics / reporting
- CRO alignment

## 3) Prioritization system

Use:
- impact
- effort
- confidence
- time-to-signal
- strategic fit

Group work into:
- quick wins
- foundational fixes
- growth bets
- experiments
- monitor only

## 4) Delivery cadence

For ongoing consulting or retainers, use a monthly rhythm:

### Week 1
- review performance
- identify focus area
- agree priorities

### Week 2
- strategy / briefs / tickets
- stakeholder alignment

### Week 3
- implementation support
- QA and unblockers

### Week 4
- reporting
- experiment readout
- next-cycle planning

## 5) Owner mapping

Always specify:
- SEO owner
- dev owner
- content owner
- design owner
- analytics owner
- local ops owner
- approver / stakeholder

## 6) Ticket-writing standard

A good ticket includes:
- issue
- affected pages/templates
- rationale
- acceptance criteria
- dependencies
- QA steps
- leading metric

## 7) Client/stakeholder communication

Translate SEO into:
- business risk
- business upside
- timeline
- owner clarity
- what to expect first
- what not to overread yet

Do not drown stakeholders in jargon if the goal is action.

## 8) Standard deliverables

This module should produce:
- audit
- roadmap
- sprint backlog
- owner map
- executive summary
- monthly report
- stakeholder memo
- implementation QA checklist
