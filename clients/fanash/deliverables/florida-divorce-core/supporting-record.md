## Scope and decision

**Recommendation:** Replace the current informational-law framing with a Florida divorce service hub that helps a prospective client understand eligibility, process choices, core decisions, and Fanash's role. Retain the existing implementation URL. No redirect, migration, canonical change, publication, or live-site edit is proposed.

Define a qualified inquiry as a tracked form, call, or intake record concerning a divorce matter the firm may accept; deduplicate across call tracking, forms, and CRM before comparison. Treat rankings, impressions, clicks, sessions, and engagement as diagnostics, not final outcomes.

## Reusable lesson recorded

**Demonstrated pattern:** A V2 child can be `Required` and `PASS — CANONICAL` while a jurisdiction makes its generic service label unavailable, or while the client's corresponding destination remains unverified or publication-unsafe. Requiredness is architecture evidence, not service, jurisdiction, or destination clearance. An `Optional` classification likewise neither requires nor independently prevents a link: exact V2 direction, reviewed client and jurisdiction evidence, and contextual usefulness control the documented decision.

Final narrow workflow convention: for every Required direct child that is qualified, excluded, or mapped to a client-specific destination, record the V2 status, client/jurisdiction evidence, decision, and repair or reactivation condition. Do not silently relabel the node, invent a URL, or treat a technically live page as publication, legal, or operational clearance.
