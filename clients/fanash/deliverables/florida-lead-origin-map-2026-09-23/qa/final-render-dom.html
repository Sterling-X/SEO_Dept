Window. The last 365 days, September 24, 2025 to September 23, 2026 inclusive, by lead creation date. 1392 deduplicated leads fall inside it. The client's most recent recorded event is 2026-09-22; any gap to the window end is pipeline lag, not a drop in volume.

Florida only. 946 of 1392 leads carry a FL number and are plotted. Excluded: 405 out-of-state, 30 with no usable phone, 11 whose exchange could not be resolved. Totals describe leads carrying a FL number, not the whole pipeline. 36 of the 405 out-of-state leads still reached hired (8.9%). That is comparable to the FL rate, which suggests many of them are Florida residents whose mobile number was issued elsewhere; the Florida book is probably larger than the plotted total.

Geocoding. Each lead's telephone exchange (NPA-NXX) was resolved to its telco rate-center city and coordinates via the public localcallingguide.com prefix registry. Billing-zone suffixes that are not places were folded into their city, and directional zones such as "Tampa North" into their city, so "Houston Suburban" counts as Houston. A rate center is where the number was issued, not where the person lives: a mobile line follows its owner, so a share of these points are approximately rather than exactly right.

Rates below 5 leads are withheld. 55 of the 88 origins hold fewer than 5 leads; they show counts but a dash for rate, because "67%" off three leads invites exactly the wrong read. Rates on fewer than 50 leads are shown as whole percents.

Hired. 83 of 946 leads (8.8%) reached hired, the deepest stage this client records. Counts are to date: leads from the most recent months have had less time to sign, so recent-heavy origins read low.
