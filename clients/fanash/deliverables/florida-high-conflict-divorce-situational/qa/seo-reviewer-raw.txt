## Blocker

### Missing court-finding qualification in mediation rule

1. **Evidence:** `workflow-input.json` says a court must not refer the case if domestic-violence history would compromise mediation. [Fla. Stat. §44.102(2)(c)](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0000-0099/0044/Sections/0044.102.html) requires that the court *find* such a history would compromise mediation.
2. **Issue:** Omitting the court-finding condition could imply a party’s request alone prevents referral.
3. **Correction:** State that, upon a party’s motion or request, the court must not refer the case if it finds the qualifying domestic-violence history would compromise mediation. Update the manifest, DOCX, and legal-verification table, then rerun legal QA and render. This blocks approval of that passage only, not the architecture or remaining draft.
