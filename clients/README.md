# Client Briefs

Store one curated brief per client at `clients/<client-slug>.md`. A brief should contain verified facts and explicit decisions needed for strategy, review, and measurement. It should not become a dump of raw exports or retrieved site content.

Do not populate client facts from guesses. Use `Unknown - verification required` when a required fact is unavailable. Cite the source and verification date for facts that can change.

```markdown

## Domain
- Canonical domain:
- Relevant subdomains or alternate domains:
- Verification source and date:

## Jurisdiction
- Jurisdiction(s) served:
- Jurisdiction-specific content or advertising constraints:
- Verification source and date:

## Verified office locations
- Office name:
  - Full address:
  - Location type: staffed physical office / other verified type
  - Verification source:
  - Last verified:

## Service-area coverage
- Areas served without a verified physical office:
- Source and last verification date:

## Services
- Service:
  - Priority:
  - Audience and intent:
  - Commercial significance:
  - Current canonical page, if verified:

## Conversion definitions
- Conversion name:
  - Exact definition:
  - Source system:
  - Funnel stage:
  - Known limitations:

## Data sources
- Source:
  - Owner or system:
  - Coverage and grain:
  - Date range or freshness:
  - Known gaps:

## Constraints
- Legal, regulatory, brand, technical, operational, budget, or capacity constraints:

## Approved decisions
- Decision:
  - Status:
  - Approver:
  - Date:
  - Rationale:

## Source references
- Source title, location or URL, owner, and access or publication date:

## Verification
- Brief owner:
- Last verification date:
- Sections still requiring verification:
```

Keep confidential exports and credentials out of briefs. Reference protected source systems or approved evidence locations instead of copying sensitive records unnecessarily.
